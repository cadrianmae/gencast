F: Listener-as-a-seat — A+C+D mix

Listener occupies one of N+1 seats around a round table. Speakers at the other seats. Math derived; never behind.

N=1 (solo guest)

azimuths: 0°

N=2 (duo)

azimuths: -30°, +30°

N=3 (panel)

azimuths: -45°, 0°, +45°

N=4 (full panel)

azimuths: -54°, -18°, +18°, +54°
The math (one line): For listener at table position 270° (south), place N speakers at table-angles 270° + (360°/(N+1))·k for k=1..N. Each speaker's azimuth from listener is then atan2(x_table, y_table + R). All speakers end up in the front half — geometric guarantee (every round-table seat has positive forward-distance from any other seat).

Why this is the A+C+D mix:
• A (front-arc only): inherited — no speaker is ever behind, by geometry.
• C (literal table): inherited — angles derive from real seat positions, not arbitrary.
• D (spreads wide as N grows): inherited — N=2 ±30°, N=4 ±54°, naturally wider with more participants.

Round-table speaker positioning — pick a geometry

N=4 speakers shown for each. Listener (orange) faces up the page. Click to choose.

AFront arc ±60°
Industry standard. All speakers in a tight front arc. Never disorienting.
Translates to any headphones · less "table" feel, more "stage"
BCenter, full 360°
Listener at table center. Speakers radiate full 360°, including directly behind.
Most immersive · weak HRTFs muddle front/back · cheap earbuds collapse "behind" into "in head"
CListener as a seat
Listener occupies one of N+1 seats around an actual table. Real geometry.
Most "table-like" feel · same disorientation risk as B · math is non-trivial
DFront semicircle ±90°
Like A but spread wider — full front semicircle. Feels more "around me," nothing behind.
Predictable, never disorienting · table-like without geometry risk · my recommended default
EConfigurable arc
Profile YAML carries arc_degrees; spread N speakers across it. 120/180/270/360.
Ship D as default · power users dial to B with one config knob · same engine, parameterized

Note: option D is essentially "A with wider arc"; option E is "a profile knob that lets you pick A/B/D at runtime."
